What you get.

A small app that keeps your model running well, without you having to think about it.

Chat built in

Talk to your model right inside EZLM. It remembers what you tell it about yourself, and you can see and edit everything it keeps.

  • Switch models any time.

    Your address, keys and connected apps stay the same. Your current model keeps answering while a new one downloads.

  • Model mode.

    Frees up memory by asking, one app at a time, whether it can close the ones you're not using. Nothing closes without your OK.

  • Keys you control.

    Give each device its own key, and rotate or revoke one without breaking the others.

Plugs into your tools

One click connects OpenCode, Hermes or DeepSeek Harness, and checks that each one works. Anything that speaks the OpenAI API works with your address and key.

Installing the beta.

This beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm the first time.

On Windows

  1. Open EZLM-Setup.exe from your downloads. Already have EZLM? Run it anyway: it updates EZLM and keeps your models.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. It installs just for you, opens EZLM at the end, and starts the scan.
  4. Something not right? In EZLM, press Report a problem (bottom left), say what happened, and send it. Reports are public on GitHub; your keys and files are never included.

On a Windows 11 PC with Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks unsigned apps outright, with no Run anyway button, so EZLM can't be installed there until it's signed.

On a Mac

  1. Open EZLM.dmg and drag EZLM into Applications.
  2. Open EZLM from Applications. Because this beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, macOS says it can't verify the app. Close that message (it may say Done or OK).
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. After that EZLM opens normally. The first time you use Free up memory, macOS asks whether EZLM may control your other apps: allow it so EZLM can ask them to quit.
  5. Something not right? In EZLM, press Report a problem (bottom left), say what happened, and send it. Reports are public on GitHub; your keys and files are never included.
